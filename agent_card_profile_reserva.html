Context Definition

Domain Definition

Manage reservations through a natural conversation with the customer.

Objectives Definition

Capture intent, offer available time slots, confirm requests, and reduce operational workload.

Environment Definition

Knowledge

  • Restaurant hours.
  • Capacity, tables, and zones.
  • Reservation rules.
  • Policies for changes and cancellations.

Tools

  • Check availability.
  • Propose alternatives.
  • Register the reservation request.
  • Modify, cancel, or escalate to staff.

Long Term Memory

  • Name, phone number, preferences, allergies, favorite zone, and reservation history.

Short Term Memory

  • Date, time, party size, occasion, notes, and the option chosen during the conversation.

Reflex Agent Type Definition

Utility-Based Agent

Reservation isn't binary: when the exact slot is unavailable, several valid alternatives exist (terrace 8:00pm extra capacity vs. indoor 8:15pm exact fit) that must be ranked, not just approved. Goal-Based only answers "is it possible?"; Utility-Based answers "which option is best?", scoring by time, zone, and capacity fit — it's the only one of the 4 architectures that ranks instead of just satisfying the goal.

Autonomy Dimension Definition

Semi-autonomous

Evaluates and recommends

Evaluates the venue's availability, determines whether a reservation can be created, and offers recommendations for available time slots.

Guides and escalates

Guides the reservation and creates a pending request, but escalates special cases to the restaurant team.

Criticality Dimension Definition

Risks & Limitations

  • Undetected difference between the recorded reservation and actual occupancy.
  • Allergies and medical conditions carry high criticality when the customer has them.
  • Possibility of invalid reservation attempts or attempts outside policy.
  • Communicating conditions unclearly without protecting the diner's experience (hallucination).

Guardrails, Evals, LLMaaJ and Controls

  • Real-time availability verification before confirming any reservation.
  • Explicit customer confirmation before creating, updating, or canceling a reservation.
  • Immediate alert to the restaurant team for any event the agent cannot resolve.
  • Full traceability between the reservation, assigned table, and status.
  • After several failed attempts, escalates to a human with the accumulated conversation text.
